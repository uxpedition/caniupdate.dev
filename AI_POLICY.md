# AI Contribution Policy

This project welcomes thoughtful use of AI tools to reduce repetitive work. Use them to help prepare a contribution, not to replace your judgment or your relationships with other contributors.

## Use AI for the repetitive work

AI can help you explore the codebase, develop ideas, write code and tests, and draft documentation, issues, and merge request descriptions. The repository's agent skills support this kind of assistance.

You do not need to rewrite an accurate, useful issue or merge request description just to make every sentence your own. Before submitting it:

- Read and understand the whole text.
- Check its claims against the code and evidence. Only report checks and results that actually happened.
- Remove filler, inaccuracies, and conclusions you do not share or cannot explain.
- Make sure it describes what you intend to propose or contribute.

AI can help organize your reasoning. It cannot supply your understanding or decide your position for you.

## Keep the conversation human

Comments, reviews, and replies are conversations with other people, not paperwork to automate. Write them yourself, especially when giving feedback, making a judgment, disagreeing, or responding to another contributor's concerns.

Genuine interaction matters more than polished wording, grammar, or spelling. A short reply in your own words is better than a polished response that does not sound like you. There is no need to polish your writing with AI to participate here.

Do not use AI to impersonate engagement, generate review opinions you have not formed yourself, or carry on a discussion on your behalf. An AI-drafted issue or merge request description is welcome; the conversation that follows should be yours.

## Own your contribution and your judgment

Use AI to explore options, but make the final decisions yourself. Before submitting a contribution:

- Understand the changes and be able to explain why they are appropriate.
- Review generated code and verify the behavior you changed.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and address feedback yourself.
- Follow the project's other contribution and verification guidance.

An AI answer is not evidence that a claim is true or a change is correct. Bring your own reasoning and the evidence you checked, not an appeal to what a model said.

## In short

- AI assistance is welcome for code, exploration, documentation, and issue or merge request drafts.
- Review and own everything you submit; keep final judgment yours.
- Write comments, reviews, and replies yourself. Genuine conversation matters more than perfect prose.
- The usual contribution guidelines still apply.

Adapted from the [Human Voice policy, version 1.0.0](https://ai-policy.dev/policies/human-voice/1.0.0), published on 2026-09-23. Unlike that policy, this project permits AI-drafted issue and merge request descriptions that contributors review, understand, and take responsibility for.
